# AI Feedback Intelligence Engine

An interactive, client-side NLP and customer feedback intelligence dashboard built with semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Features
- **Automated Sentiment Scoring:** Classifies customer reviews and support tickets into Positive, Neutral, or Negative with confidence percentages.
- **Topic & Entity Extraction:** Automatically tags feedback by business domain (`Performance & Speed`, `UI / UX`, `Customer Support`, `Pricing & Billing`, `AI Accuracy & Features`).
- **Urgency Triage:** Flags critical bugs, crashes, and churn risks as **High Priority**.
- **Next-Best-Action Recommendations:** Generates tailored operational actions for engineering, support, or product teams.
- **Live KPI Analytics & JSON Export:** Tracks Net Sentiment Score, recurring themes, sentiment distribution, and exports structured reports as JSON.
